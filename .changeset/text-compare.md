New package: compare two texts or files by line, word or character, with ignore options, folded unchanged lines, navigation between changes, merges from one side to the other, a unified diff checked against `git diff`, and a worker for large texts. `@web-kit/ui` passes a dropped or opened file's name to `onText`, takes `Alt` and key names such as `ArrowDown` and `F7` in hotkeys, gives `Menu` a field look and items that keep it open, and lets `ToolMenu` list a tool's own items.
